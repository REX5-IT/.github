# REX5

**IT services for remote agencies | Managed access, SSO & security**

REX5 keeps employee and freelancer access organized as teams grow. We manage access to business software, cloud platforms, and hosting accounts, working with your designated approvers and IT team.

Our Managed Access Operations service includes:

- Account onboarding and offboarding.
- Permissions and regular access reviews.
- Single sign-on (SSO) and multifactor authentication (MFA).
- Initial access cleanup, ongoing account administration, and documented procedures.

You retain ownership of your systems, subscriptions, and decisions.

Founded by Sebastian Sejzer, REX5 draws on more than 20 years of experience in IT infrastructure, security automation, and technical operations.
